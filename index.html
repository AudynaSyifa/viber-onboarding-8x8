Important Policy Change — Effective July 15, 2026

Viber now requires all transactional and OTP messages to use pre-approved message templates. Sending free-text transactional messages is no longer allowed. Key impacts:

  • All OTP (one-time password) messages must use an approved OTP template containing a {{pin}} variable — otherwise messages may be charged at the higher Promotional rate
  • All order confirmations, shipping updates, account notifications must use an approved Transactional template
  • Templates require Viber human review — approval takes up to 24 hours
  • Any change to a template requires full resubmission and a new 24-hour review cycle
What is Viber?
Viber Business Messaging
Viber is a messaging and calling platform with over 1 billion registered users, widely used across Southeast Asia, Eastern Europe, and the Middle East. For businesses, Viber offers Business Messaging through verified Official Accounts — allowing brands to send authenticated, transactional notifications directly to customers inside the Viber app.

Messages are sent via a dedicated Business Account (identified by a Service ID), and are displayed with your business name and logo. Viber Business Messaging is ideal for transactional notifications and OTP delivery — not promotional campaigns. As of July 2026, all transactional messages must use pre-approved templates registered with Viber.

✅ What Viber Business is for

  • OTP / one-time password delivery
  • Order confirmations & shipping updates
  • Payment and transaction alerts
  • Account notifications & security alerts
  • Appointment reminders
  • Service status notifications

💡 Key advantages

  • Rich message display — business name, logo, and verified badge visible to recipients
  • OTP Copy button — OTP templates show a native copy-to-clipboard button
  • Two-way messaging — optional for customer support flows
  • High deliverability — direct integration, no carrier routing
  • Multi-language support — separate template per language
New Policy
Viber Message Templates
All transactional and OTP messages must now use pre-approved templates. Here's what you need to know before designing your templates.
📝
Two Template Categories

OTP — for one-time passwords. Must include {{pin}} variable. Shows a native Copy button in Viber app.
Transactional — for order updates, alerts, notifications.

✏️
Body Rules

1–875 characters. Must start and end with static text (not a variable). No two variables next to each other without text between them.

🔢
Variables

Max 5 variables per template. Variable names: English only, max 50 characters. No URLs allowed inside variable values at send time.

🌐
One Language Per Template

Each template supports one language only. To support multiple languages (e.g. English + Thai), submit separate templates for each.

⏱️
Approval Time

Viber human moderators review each template. Standard approval takes up to 24 hours. Plan accordingly before go-live.

🔒
Templates Are Immutable

Once approved, templates cannot be edited. Any change requires deleting the template and resubmitting — starting a new 24-hour review cycle.

Step by Step
Onboarding Process
Steps for setting up a new Viber Business Account via 8x8.
1
Client

Complete Viber Onboarding Document & Prepare Assets

Fill out the Viber Onboarding Document provided by 8x8 and prepare all required assets:

  • Business name, category, registered country, launch date
  • Logo images in all 5 required dimensions
  • Confirm one-way or two-way messaging
  • Signed Warranty Letter
Client Action
2
Client

Prepare & Submit Template Drafts to 8x8

Design your message templates for all required use cases and submit them to 8x8 for review. Ensure all templates follow Viber's rules:

  • OTP templates must contain {{pin}} variable
  • Body starts and ends with static text
  • No two variables placed next to each other
  • Include example values for each variable
⚠️ Templates with promotional content will be declined by Viber. Review carefully before submission. Any change after approval = delete + full resubmission.
Client Action
3
Agency (8x8)

Create Viber Business Account

8x8 submits the business registration to the Viber Business Portal using the onboarding document and assets provided. The business name is registered and logo uploaded.

ℹ️ Your business name is checked against the Viber app before submission. If it already exists, 8x8 will coordinate accordingly.
8x8 Action
4
Viber Team

Account Review & Approval

Viber reviews the business account submission. Standard approval takes approximately 5–7 working days. Special industries (gambling, lending) may take longer as additional documents are reviewed by Viber's legal team.

⚠️ Do not proceed to template submission until the business account is Approved/Active in the Viber portal.
Viber Action
5
Agency (8x8)

Submit Templates to Viber for Approval

Once the account is approved, 8x8 submits your message templates to Viber for human review. Each template enters a Pending state. You will be notified when templates are Approved or Declined.

  • Approval: template ready for use immediately
  • Declined: reason provided — template must be corrected and resubmitted
ℹ️ Template review takes up to 24 hours per submission. Submit all templates at once to avoid delays.
8x8 Action
6
Agency (8x8)

Channel Configuration & API Setup

8x8 configures your Viber channel and provides you with the API credentials and approved template IDs (templateKeys) needed to integrate.

8x8 Action
7
Client

Integration & Testing

  • Test OTP delivery with Copy button functionality
  • Validate template parameter mapping
  • Confirm message display (name, logo) on recipient's device
Client Action

Agency (8x8)

Go Live 🎉

8x8 sets the account to Live and billing begins. Your Viber Business Account is now active and sending in production.
