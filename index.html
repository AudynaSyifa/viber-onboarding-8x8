FAQ & Resources
Common questions and useful links for your Viber onboarding journey.

What is the BM Warranty Letter and why do I need to sign it?

It's a legal agreement between your company and Viber Media S.à r.l. By signing, you confirm compliance with Viber's Business Messages Guidelines — only sending to opted-in users, no spam, and handling complaints within 7 days. It's mandatory before Viber approves your sender profile.

What is the difference between 1-way and 2-way messaging?

1-way (outbound only): You send to customers — OTP, alerts, transactional notifications. Customers cannot reply. Best for high-volume automated sends.

2-way (conversational): Customers can reply to your messages. Best for support, engagement, and customer service use cases.

How long does Viber's sender approval take?

Typically 5–10 business days after 8x8 submits your complete registration. The timeline starts only after all documents (form, warranty letter, business license, logos) have been received and verified. Incomplete or partial submissions will restart the review clock.

What happens if I send messages without a registered template?

As of July 15, 2026, Viber requires pre-approved templates for all transactional and OTP messages. Messages sent without a registered template are reclassified and billed at the promotional rate (higher). There is no failed delivery — the impact is on your invoice. Your CSM will help you register templates as part of onboarding.

Can I use my existing Viber account for Business Messages?

No. Viber Business Messages requires a dedicated sender profile created through 8x8's partnership with Viber. This is a separate, verified sender — different from a personal Viber account.

Do I need a developer to integrate?

If you use API integration — yes, you'll need a developer to configure webhooks and integrate the 8x8 API into your system. If you use the 8x8 Connect Portal for manual sends or basic automation — no developer is required. Discuss with your CSM during the kick-off call.
